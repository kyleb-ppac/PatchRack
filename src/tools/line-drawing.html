Photo → squared-up line drawing for a gear definition

1 Photo and faceplate size

Drop a photo of the front or rear panel here, paste one (Cmd/Ctrl+V), or
JPG, PNG, iPhone HEIC, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, GIF, BMP and SVG all work.

Pick the same height and width as the gear definition in PatchRack. The drawing comes out exactly that shape, so it drops into the faceplate with nothing stretched.

2 Square it up

  1. Drag the four amber corners onto the four corners of the faceplate (include the rack ears for rack gear). The labels say which corner is which.
  2. Line the dashed guides up with straight things on the panel (rows of jacks, the top edge) to check.
  3. Click a corner, then use the arrow keys to nudge it (Shift = bigger steps). Drag inside the box to move all four.

Click a shape to select it (Shift-click for more, or drag a box around several). Drag to move, drag the small squares to resize, arrow keys nudge (Shift = bigger). Shapes snap to line up with each other. Sizes are in mm on the real faceplate. V R C L T pick tools, Del deletes, Esc deselects.

Paint out areas

Paint over handles, knobs, cables, reflections or anything else you don't want in the drawing. Paint on either picture below. Painted areas are left empty, and they don't count when the tool decides what a strong line is, so the rest of the panel can come out with more detail. Painting stays put if you move the corners. Esc stops painting.
